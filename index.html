[ 02 ] AboutRay

I care about the part most people never notice: how an interface responds, how it moves, and what happens at the edges.

I make native Mac apps in Swift and SwiftUI, desktop and web tools in Rust and TypeScript, and games on Roblox in Luau.

Doing both the design and the engineering means nothing gets lost in a handoff. I prototype until something feels right in the hand, then test it until I can prove it.
